## Q2 highlights

NovaChip Technologies reported Q2 2026 revenue of **$1.22 billion**, up 7% year over year but below the company's internal plan. Adjusted gross margin was **56.1%**, compared with 58.4% in the prior-year quarter. Adjusted EPS was **$1.41**.

Data-center accelerator demand remained strong, but shipments were constrained by export-license timing and a slower-than-expected qualification cycle for the company's next-generation accelerator platform.

## Full-year outlook

Management **lowered its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to $5.0-$5.2 billion from the prior $5.4-$5.6 billion range**.

The company also revised its expected full-year adjusted gross margin to **55%-56%**, compared with the previous expectation of approximately 58%.

Management said the revised outlook primarily reflects:
- delayed export approvals for certain high-end products,
- a two-quarter shift in one hyperscale customer ramp, and
- a less favorable product mix during the second half of 2026.

The company said it continues to expect healthy long-term AI infrastructure demand and does not view the delays as a loss of strategic customer relationships.
