## Q2 highlights

Apex Retail Group reported Q2 revenue of **$3.45 billion**. Comparable-store sales increased **4.8%**, led by grocery, household essentials, and membership income. Adjusted EPS was **$1.82**.

## Full-year outlook

Apex **reaffirmed** its existing 2026 outlook.

Management continues to expect:
- full-year revenue growth of **4%-6%**,
- comparable-store sales growth of **3%-5%**, and
- adjusted EPS of **$7.10-$7.40**.

Management noted modest pressure from freight costs but said productivity initiatives and private-label mix are offsetting the impact.
