## Prepared remarks

**Elena Park, CEO:** We entered Q2 with very strong demand for our accelerator products. However, two operational items affected the timing of revenue recognition: export-license approvals and qualification timing at one large hyperscale customer.

**Daniel Cho, CFO:** Q2 revenue was $1.22 billion and adjusted gross margin was 56.1%. Because the shipment timing issues extend into the second half, we are changing our full-year expectations.

**Daniel Cho, CFO:** **We now expect 2026 revenue of $5.0 billion to $5.2 billion, down from our previous range of $5.4 billion to $5.6 billion.** We also expect adjusted gross margin of 55% to 56%, versus our prior view of approximately 58%.

## Analyst Q&A

**Analyst:** Is the lower outlook primarily a demand issue?

**Elena Park, CEO:** No. We continue to see strong end-market demand. The change is mostly timing. Some products cannot ship until licenses are approved, and one major customer moved part of its deployment from Q4 2026 into early 2027.

**Analyst:** Should investors assume the delayed revenue is lost?

**Daniel Cho, CFO:** We are not assuming the revenue disappears. Our planning assumption is that much of it shifts, although timing remains uncertain.

**Analyst:** What would cause another change to guidance?

**Elena Park, CEO:** The biggest variables are export approvals, customer qualification schedules, and product mix.
