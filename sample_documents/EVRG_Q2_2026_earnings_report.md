## Q2 highlights

Evergreen Energy Systems reported Q2 revenue of **$2.08 billion** and adjusted EBITDA of **$385 million**. Solar equipment shipments grew year over year, while utility-scale project revenue was affected by construction delays in Texas and Arizona.

## Full-year outlook

The company **reduced 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance to $1.60-$1.70 billion from $1.80-$1.90 billion**.

Management cited:
- approximately $110 million of expected EBITDA shifting into 2027 because of project commissioning delays,
- higher financing costs, and
- temporary logistics expenses related to inverter component shortages.

Full-year revenue expectations were narrowed to $8.4-$8.7 billion.

Management stated that contracted backlog remains strong and that the delayed projects have not been cancelled.
